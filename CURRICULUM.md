# Learning Quest: curriculum map

For each level: the learning outcome, the success criteria the child sees, and links to the NCF for Foundational Stage 2022 (NCF-FS), the NCERT Learning Outcomes (2017) and the current NCERT Class 2 textbooks. Codes and quotes were checked against the official PDFs. Confidence shows how closely each level matches.

Sources: [NCF-FS 2022](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/NCF_for_Foundational_Stage_20_October_2022.pdf) · [NCERT Learning Outcomes 2017](https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/publication/otherpublications/tilops101.pdf) · [NIPUN Bharat](https://dsel.education.gov.in/nipun-bharat) · [Chrysalis Thinkers](https://chrysalis.world/products/thinkers) · [LEGO Foundation: Learning through play](https://humanities.uct.ac.za/media/316884)

## How the game teaches

**Chrysalis lesson flow.** Chrysalis describes lessons that move through engagement, concept-building, assessment and reflection. In the game, these are: a story world and a spoken learning goal; picture models and hints; 10 questions checked against the success criteria; and "How did that feel?" at the end. Chrysalis publishes only this outline, so the mapping is light. It can be aligned more closely with Chrysalis's internal framework documents.

**Play-based learning (NCF-FS 1.4).** The game builds in choice, wonder and joy; guided play with hints instead of answers; try, test and retry with no timer; meaningful Indian and Tamil Nadu contexts; and short sessions.

**Home language.** Instructions, hints and praise are spoken in Tamil, while English and Hindi words are spoken in their own voices (NCF-FS 3.2).

## Maths

All 40 Maths levels map to NCF-FS CG-8 (competencies C-8.2 to C-8.13). The NCERT 2017 Class II Mathematics outcomes cover two-digit numbers, place value, addition and subtraction, money up to Rs. 100, shapes, non-standard measurement, and days and months. Joyful Mathematics 2 has clear chapters for most worlds. Several levels sit below Class 2 (single-digit facts, counting to 20) and work as warm-ups. Some sit above it or outside it: cm with a ruler, half past, odd/even, and the table of 4 as a standalone table.

## English

For a Tamil-speaking child, English is a second language (L2). Phonics and reading levels therefore map to NCF-FS CG-11 (C-11.1, C-11.2), and oral and vocabulary levels map to CG-9 (C-9.x). NCERT's own 'About the Book' for Mridang 2 maps that book to C-9.1 to C-9.7 and C-11.1. The NCERT 2017 Class II English outcomes match the describing-words, pronoun, sentence-writing and comprehension levels exactly. They say nothing about phonics, plurals or nouns. NIPUN Bharat Class II covers naming words, action words and punctuation.

In a Tamil-speaking home, Hindi is the child's L2 or L3, not L1.
